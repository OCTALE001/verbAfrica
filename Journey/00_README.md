# Journey

*Maintained by Alexander October + Claude | Started June 2026*

---

## What This Folder Is

This is the **story** of building verbAfrica. Not a working document — a record of what happened, what was decided, what was learned, and why.

Future-me will read this to remember how this came together. Future cohort members might read it to learn how this kind of thing actually gets built. Someday it might become content — a how-to, a talk, a book chapter, whatever.

It is **explicitly separate** from working docs (`Strategy/`, `Operations/`, `Cohort/`, `data/`). Those are the *artifacts*. This is the *story behind* the artifacts.

---

## How It Gets Updated

**By Claude, during every verbAfrica session.** When something meaningful happens — a decision, a milestone, a piece of feedback, a moment of *"oh, that's interesting"* — Claude appends an entry to `Storyline.md`. Newest entries at the top.

**By a scheduled task every few days.** A scheduled remote agent reviews recent activity (file changes, commits) and catches up the Storyline with anything that wasn't captured in real-time.

**By me, whenever.** If I have thoughts I want recorded — a conversation with someone, an observation, a moment of doubt or excitement — I can either tell Claude or write directly into `Storyline.md` myself.

---

## What Counts as "Meaningful"

**Worth recording:**
- Decisions that shaped the direction
- Things that surprised me / changed my mind
- Conversations with cohort members or clients that taught us something
- Moments when something *worked* (or didn't)
- Mistakes, dead ends, and what we learned from them
- The *thinking* behind decisions, not just the decisions themselves

**Not worth recording:**
- Routine work logs ("today I edited the bio of creative X") — those belong in git, not here
- Minutiae no one would ever care about

**When in doubt:** would future-me, reading this in a year, want to know? If yes, write it.
