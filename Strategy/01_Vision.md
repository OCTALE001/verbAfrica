# Vision

*Last updated: 2026-06-05 — Full v1 draft from founder interview.*

---

> **One-line version:** *verbAfrica makes it easy to book South Africa's best creatives — and gives those creatives a tech edge no one else can offer them.*

---

## 1. The Company

We help people book curated **cohort members** — vetted creatives and service providers — for events.

The eventual product is a **multi-provider event builder**: a single booking flow where a client can configure a whole event end-to-end. Book a band *and* pick the setlist. Book a photographer *and* choose the shotlist. One booking, multiple providers, granular creative decisions in the client's hands.

**Two design principles** sit underneath this:
- **Put decisions in the client's hands.** Most agencies and marketplaces ask the client to *brief* and then wait. We let the client *configure* — directly, in the flow.
- **Facilitate the supply side.** The creatives don't have to chase, scope, or admin. The platform handles the process so they can focus on the work.

This is what makes us different from:
- **Open marketplaces** (anyone can sign up) — we are curated; the cohort is the product.
- **Directory sites** (browse and contact outside) — bookings happen *inside* the platform.
- **Event agencies** (humans plan the whole thing) — clients drive their own event, with our infrastructure underneath.

---

## 2. Why Booking Admin First

Booking admin is the **wedge**, not the destination.

It's the easiest way to get inside how the cohort actually works — to do a real gap analysis on where each creative is, how they operate, what slows them down. Solving booking earns the trust and visibility to see *every other* place tech could step-change a creative's output.

It's also already validated on both sides:
- **Demand:** real clients are asking to book these creatives for events.
- **Need:** cohort members don't have the resources or time to do the "shadow work" — the unpaid back-and-forth, scheduling, contracts, follow-ups — that booking currently requires of them.

So booking isn't "the biggest creative problem." It's the **door** — a real problem with real demand, whose solution opens the path to everything else.

---

## 3. verbAfrica's Role in the Bigger Company

The company (working name: **verbAfrica**) is a **creative-industry tech-enablement studio**. The booking platform is product #1 — it's the entry point, not the whole offering.

The cohort relationship is symbiotic by design:

**→ What cohort members get:**
- **Bookings** — through the platform, solving the admin pain.
- **A tech edge their peers don't have** — projection mapping, 3D printing, AI augmentation, VR/AR. Practical creative tools their industry can't usually access.
- **Brand and business foundations** — the basics that turn a creative into a sustainable practice.

**→ What the company gets:**
- **Trust and visibility** — deep insight into how each creative actually works, where they get stuck, where they could leap.
- **Real-world R&D** — every tech intervention on a cohort member's work refines a service we can later offer more broadly.
- **Buy-in** — cohort members who feel the value become the proof, the referrers, and the case studies.

**The unfair advantage:** Alexander is an engineer with access to manufacturing and design capability. The company exists at the intersection of *existing technology* and *the creative industry* — a bridge most agencies and most engineers can't build because they sit on one side only.

**Future product directions** (signal, not commitment):
- **Event curation** — the multi-provider event-builder described in §1.
- **Tech-enablement as a service** — what we refine through the cohort pilot becomes an offering to creatives beyond the cohort.

---

## 4. What Success Looks Like in 6 Months

**By December 2026, success means every cohort member can honestly say:**

> *"I'm getting bookings through verbAfrica. verbAfrica has also helped me build a strong business foundation, and levelled up my game by enabling my art and vision through tech."*

Three concrete strands underneath that:

1. **Bookings work.** Real bookings have been completed for cohort members through the platform, with real test clients. (Loose target: a handful of completed bookings spread across the cohort — to be tightened in the Roadmap.)
2. **Cohort members are professionalised.** All 10 have a brand guide and basic business / creative documents attached to their profiles.
3. **Each member has a tech edge.** Every cohort member has received at least one tech intervention (projection mapping, 3D printing, AI, VR/AR, or otherwise) — and ideally one their peers don't have.

We will revisit and tighten the specific numbers in `02_Roadmap.md` as the beta starts running.

---

## 5. What We Are Not Doing

The things we are explicitly saying **no** to — for now:

- **Not open registration.** Curated cohort only, by invitation.
- **Not a label, management, or PR agency.** We're booking and tech infrastructure, not representation.
- **Not international.** **Johannesburg first**, then the rest of South Africa.

This list will grow as we get pulled toward things that don't serve the vision. Add to it freely.
