# Response Standards

*v1 — 2026-06-09 | Internal-only — not public-facing*

---

## Why This Doc Is Internal

We deliberately **do not publish a response-time promise** on the platform during the beta. We're not staffing a 24/7 inbox; a public commitment we'd miss on a bad day would burn credibility faster than not making one.

But we still need targets *for ourselves* — so we know when we're falling behind, and so we can decide whether to add capacity or change how we work.

Revisit this doc in Phase 5 review: by then we'll know our real cadence and can decide whether to publicise an SLA.

---

## Internal Targets

| What | Target | Never Miss |
|---|---|---|
| First response to a new enquiry | **24 hours** | 48 hours |
| Quote roster back to client after assembly | **72 hours** | 5 days |
| Confirmation to all sides once both yes | **Same day** | 24 hours |
| Post-gig feedback request sent | **24 hours after gig** | 3 days |

These are conservative on purpose. We're optimising for *never missing the never-miss column* over hitting the target column every time.

---

## When We Miss

1. **Log it in the sheet.** Fill the `Delay reason` column for that booking with a short note (e.g. *"travelling — laptop closed"*, *"forgot to follow up"*, *"waited on cohort response"*).
2. **Notice the pattern.** If the same kind of delay shows up twice, it's not bad luck — it's structural. Note in `Journey/Storyline.md`.
3. **Decide in the weekly check-in.** Either the target is wrong (raise it, with reason), or we change how we work (add a reminder, batch responses, etc.).

---

## What "Response" Means

A response is **a useful update** to the client or cohort, not just acknowledging receipt.

✅ Counts as response:
- "Received your enquiry, I'll come back to you within X with a roster."
- "Confirmed Shivolski is available — here's pricing."
- "Need a few more details before I can match — could you tell me Y?"

❌ Does not count:
- A read-receipt or 👍 emoji.
- "Got it, thanks" with no commitment to a follow-up time.
