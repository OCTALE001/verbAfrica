## The Workflow — How to Keep This Current

### Weekly (15–20 min with Claude)

Once a week, open a conversation with Claude and paste:
- Any notes from creative or client conversations you had
- Any articles or industry news you came across
- Any new competitors or platforms you heard about
- Any product decisions you're considering

Ask Claude to: synthesise what you brought, update any relevant documents, flag anything that changes existing assumptions.

### After Every Research Conversation

After each creative or client interview:
1. Write rough notes immediately (even voice note to text is fine)
2. Bring them to Claude with: "Help me pull the key insights from these notes and flag anything that changes our hypotheses in document 04"
3. Update `04_Key_Insights_and_Open_Questions.md` with what changed

### Monthly Deep Dive

Once a month, ask Claude to:
- Run fresh research on any new competitors that have emerged
- Check if any of your open questions from document 04 can now be answered from desk research
- Identify any industry news that changes the landscape
- Suggest what the next 30 days of research should focus on

### Before Any Major Decision

Before writing the business plan, setting the roadmap, or pitching to anyone — ask Claude:
> "Based on everything in the verbAfrica Research folder, what are the biggest gaps in our knowledge right now, and what should we be most cautious about?"

## How to Use Claude as a Research Partner

Claude works best when you treat it as a thinking partner, not a search engine. Here's the difference:

**Search engine mode (less useful):**
> "What is the SA gig economy worth?"

**Thinking partner mode (more useful):**
> "I just talked to a Jhb photographer who said she gets 90% of her work through referrals and has never tried any platform. What does that tell us about our go-to-market? Does it change anything we've assumed?"

The second prompt brings your real-world observation and asks Claude to connect it to strategy. That's where the insight lives.

**Other useful prompts:**
- "I'm about to DM this person [share their profile]. Help me write a personalised opener based on their work."
- "Here are my notes from 5 creative interviews. What patterns do you see? What surprised you?"
- "I read this article [paste link or text]. What's relevant for verbAfrica?"
- "A potential investor asked me [question]. How should I answer based on what we know?"
- "We're considering [product decision]. What does the research suggest about whether this is the right call?"
