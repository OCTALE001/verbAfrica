How verbAfrica Works

We help South African brands and event organisers find and book the right creatives.

Browse the Directory
Browse South African creatives you can actually book. Filter by category, budget and location, and see real rates on every profile.
Shortlist Your Picks
Save the profiles you like to a shortlist, then compare their rates, skills and past work before you commit.
Post a Brief
Tell us about your project, timeline and budget, and we'll show you the creatives who fit.
Connect & Book
Send an enquiry straight from any profile, agree on the details, and lock in your booking.
