How verbAfrica Works

We connect South African brands and event organisers with a curated network of top-tier creatives — fast, transparent, and on-brief.

1
🔍
Browse the Directory
Explore 15+ vetted South African creatives. Filter by category, budget and location. Read bios, see real rates and highlights.
2
♡
Shortlist Your Picks
Save profiles you love to your shortlist. Compare them side by side — rates, skills and notable work — before committing.
3
📋
Post a Brief
Describe your project, timeline and budget. Our matching engine instantly surfaces the best-fit creatives for your brief.
4
💬
Connect & Book
Send an enquiry directly from any profile. Receive proposals, align on scope and lock in your booking — all in one place.
